Dealer.com Is Dropping Our Features

Group summary Dealer.com Partnership Audit

DDC's significant VDP errors are reducing DAG's business potential.

Between October 22 and October 27, 2026, we performed a comprehensive audit where we compared our export vs what DDC presents on our VDPs. Our merchandising team recorded and exported 52,871 shopper feature names on 535 used cars. Dolan tracks the Top 10% of the most valuable shopper attributes, which are 8,599 hot features. Our audit found that Dealer.com dropped 1,191 of the most important, hot features from the vehicle pages (14%). The missing packages and optionally installed items totaled $54,300 at factory MSRP.

The search page is worse. Our team's export names 7,681 hot shopper features that pair with Dealer.com's own search filters. The DDC filters do not show 1,962 of them (26%). 150 of 523 cars on our website have 5 or more features that a shopper cannot find with those filters. A shopper who filters for what they want does not see our car.
