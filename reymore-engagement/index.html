Method and data notes

How to read this report

Who is countedPeople in the United States who opened at least one vehicle page or one inventory list page on reymore.com between Aug 11 and Sep 7, 2026. Visitors who only saw the homepage or a service page are not counted. Known vendor tools and bots are removed.
How a color is setBy what the shopper did across all visits in the 28 days. Red Hot wins over Hot, Hot over Warm, Warm over Cool. Red Hot = a call, text, form, chat, test-drive or directions request. Hot = a second visit, or a payment, trade, CARFAX or save-the-car action, or 40+ photos. Warm = filters, 2+ cars or 20+ photos in one visit. Cool = the rest who opened a car. Ice Cold = list only.
Where a shopper "came from"The source of the shopper's first recorded visit, as Google Analytics labels it. Edmunds and Cars.com are labeled "referral" by their links; the page that actually sent the click was Facebook or Instagram 96–98% of the time.
Counts and roundingGoogle Analytics estimates user counts, so rows can overlap by about 1%. Each row is rounded to sum to 100%. Rows under 100 shoppers are shown grey and never win a highlight.
SpendSeptember 2026 billing as supplied by the store. Cars Social is a separate $1,000 product from the Cars.com listing. CarGurus listing, CARFAX and Autotrader show 29 tracked click-throughs between them; the vendors' own click reports were not part of this report.
What this report does not doIt does not connect website shoppers to leads or sales, and it does not look at where shoppers live. It reads the website only.
